Q01. What is 15% of 200?
     A) 20
     B) 25
     C) 30
     D) 35
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 30

Q02. If a train travels at 60 km/h, how far will it travel in 2.5 hours?
     A) 120 km
     B) 140 km
     C) 150 km
     D) 160 km
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 150 km

     A) 5 days
     B) 6 days
     C) 7.5 days
     D) 8 days
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 6 days

SECTION 2: MECHANICAL ENGINEERING - CREO SPECIALIZATION (QUESTIONS 21 - 30)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q21. What is PTC Creo primarily used for in engineering industries?
     A) 3D CAD parametric modeling, simulation, and design
     B) Relational database management
     C) Website frontend coding
     D) Sound editing and audio mastering
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) 3D CAD parametric modeling, simulation, and design

Q22. In 3D CAD modeling, which tool is used to create a 3D solid by projecting a 2D sketch perpendicular to the sketch plane?
     A) Extrude
     B) Revolve
     C) Sweep
     D) Blend
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Extrude

Q23. Which CAD feature creates a rotational symmetrical solid by rotating a 2D cross-section around a center axis?
     A) Revolve
     B) Chamfer
     C) Shell
     D) Pattern
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Revolve

Q24. What is a 'Datum' in Creo (such as a Datum Plane, Datum Axis, or Datum Point)?
     A) A reference geometry used to construct sketches, features, and assemblies
     B) A measurement of material cost
     C) A file compression format
     D) The physical weight of the finished part
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) A reference geometry used to construct sketches, features, and assemblies

Q25. What is the primary engineering purpose of adding a 'Fillet' (Round) to internal corners of a mechanical part?
     A) Reduce stress concentration and eliminate sharp corners
     B) Increase the overall weight of the part
     C) Make the part easier to paint
     D) Speed up 3D file saving
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Reduce stress concentration and eliminate sharp corners

Q26. In a tensile test of mild steel, what is the point on the stress-strain curve beyond which permanent plastic deformation occurs?
     A) Yield Point
     B) Proportional Limit
     C) Breaking Point
     D) Ultimate Tensile Strength
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Yield Point

Q27. What does GD&T stand for in mechanical engineering drafting and manufacturing?
     A) Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing
     B) General Drafting and Testing
     C) Global Design and Tooling
     D) Graphical Dimensioning Technique
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing

Q28. What is the primary function of Finite Element Analysis (FEA) software in mechanical design?
     A) Simulating structural stresses, deformations, and thermal behaviors under load
     B) Generating barcodes for inventory boxes
     C) Writing CNC machine operator pay slips
     D) Checking spelling in drafting notes
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Simulating structural stresses, deformations, and thermal behaviors under load

Q29. Which CAD feature is used to hollow out a solid model leaving walls of specified uniform thickness?
     A) Shell
     B) Draft
     C) Rib
     D) Hole
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Shell

Q30. Which casting defect is caused by trapped gas bubbles inside the molten metal during solidification?
     A) Blowhole / Porosity
     B) Hot Tear
     C) Cold Shut
     D) Mismatch
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Blowhole / Porosity
