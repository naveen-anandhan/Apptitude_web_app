SECTION 1: GENERAL APTITUDE (QUESTIONS 01 - 20)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q01. What is 15% of 200?
     A) 30
     B) 35
     C) 20
     D) 25
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) 30

Q02. If a train travels at 60 km/h, how far will it travel in 2.5 hours?
     A) 140 km
     B) 160 km
     C) 150 km
     D) 120 km
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 150 km

Q03. If A can complete a work in 10 days and B in 15 days, how many days will they take working together?
     A) 8 days
     B) 5 days
     C) 7.5 days
     D) 6 days
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) 6 days

Q04. A shopkeeper buys an item for ₹400 and sells it for ₹500. What is the profit percentage?
     A) 30%
     B) 15%
     C) 25%
     D) 20%
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 25%

Q05. The ratio of two numbers is 3:5 and their sum is 80. What is the smaller number?
     A) 32
     B) 36
     C) 24
     D) 30
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) 30

Q06. Find the simple interest on ₹5,000 at 6% per annum for 2 years.
     A) ₹600
     B) ₹550
     C) ₹500
     D) ₹650
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) ₹600

Q07. The average of five numbers is 20. If one number is removed, the average becomes 18. What was the removed number?
     A) 26
     B) 28
     C) 30
     D) 24
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 28

Q08. Find the next number in the sequence: 3, 6, 12, 24, 48, ?
     A) 108
     B) 84
     C) 72
     D) 96
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) 96

Q09. Find the next number in the sequence: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?
     A) 39
     B) 35
     C) 37
     D) 41
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 37

Q10. If 'LIGHT' is coded as 'MTHIU', how is 'PLANT' coded following the same pattern?
     A) QMANS
     B) QNCPO
     C) QMBNU
     D) QMBOU
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) QMBOU

Q11. Pointing to a photograph, a man said: 'She is the daughter of my father's only son.' How is the girl related to the man?
     A) Mother
     B) Daughter
     C) Niece
     D) Sister
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) Daughter

Q12. A person walks 10 meters North, turns right and walks 10 meters, then turns right and walks 10 meters. In which direction is he from the starting point?
     A) North
     B) South
     C) West
     D) East
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) East

Q13. Which word does NOT belong with the others?
     A) Yard
     B) Centimeter
     C) Ounce
     D) Inch
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) Ounce

Q14. Choose the correct synonym for 'CANDID':
     A) Secretive
     B) Frank and honest
     C) Dishonest
     D) Fearful
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) Frank and honest

Q15. Choose the correctly spelled word:
     A) Ocurrence
     B) Occurrance
     C) Occurrence
     D) Occurence
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) Occurrence

Q16. What is the primary function of RAM in a computer?
     A) Temporary volatile storage for active processes
     B) Permanent data storage
     C) Power regulation
     D) Cooling the motherboard
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Temporary volatile storage for active processes

Q17. Which of the following is an Operating System?
     A) Oracle SQL
     B) Linux
     C) Google Chrome
     D) Adobe Acrobat
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) Linux

Q18. What does HTTP stand for?
     A) HyperText Transfer Protocol
     B) HomeText Translation Process
     C) HighText Transmission Program
     D) HyperTransfer Terminal Protocol
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) HyperText Transfer Protocol

Q19. How many bits are there in 1 Byte?
     A) 16
     B) 8
     C) 4
     D) 32
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 8

Q20. In a flowchart, which geometric symbol is used to represent a decision/condition?
     A) Diamond
     B) Rectangle
     C) Parallelogram
     D) Oval
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Diamond

SECTION 2: LOGICAL & VERBAL REASONING (QUESTIONS 21 - 30)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q21. Which planet is commonly known as the Red Planet?
     A) Venus
     B) Saturn
     C) Mars
     D) Jupiter
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) Mars

Q22. In how many ways can the letters of the word 'LEAD' be arranged?
     A) 24
     B) 48
     C) 16
     D) 12
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) 24

Q23. What is the probability of getting an even number when rolling a standard six-sided die?
     A) 1/3
     B) 1/2
     C) 2/3
     D) 1/6
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 1/2

Q24. A clock shows 3:00. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand?
     A) 60 degrees
     B) 75 degrees
     C) 120 degrees
     D) 90 degrees
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) 90 degrees

Q25. Complete the analogy: Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?
     A) Pen
     B) School
     C) Book
     D) Student
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) School

Q26. Which gas is most abundant in the Earth's atmosphere?
     A) Nitrogen
     B) Oxygen
     C) Carbon Dioxide
     D) Hydrogen
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Nitrogen

Q27. Select the antonym for 'EXPAND':
     A) Enlarge
     B) Stretch
     C) Shrink
     D) Extend
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) Shrink

Q28. Which of the following is a cybersecurity practice to prevent unauthorized access?
     A) Using simple birthdays as passwords
     B) Enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
     C) Clicking unknown links in emails
     D) Disabling system firewalls
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) Enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Q29. If 12 pens cost ₹180, what is the cost of 7 pens?
     A) ₹100
     B) ₹95
     C) ₹105
     D) ₹110
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) ₹105

Q30. What is the primary unit of heredity in living organisms?
     A) Tissue
     B) Protein
     C) Cell
     D) Gene
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) Gene
