SECTION 1: LEVEL 1 – BASIC APTITUDE (QUESTIONS 01 - 10)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q01. If PEN is coded as QFO (each letter moved one step forward), how is INK coded?
     A) JOL
     B) JNL
     C) IOL
     D) JOK
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) JOL
     Explanation: I→J, N→O, K→L, so INK = JOL.

Q02. Pointing to a girl, Ravi says, "She is the daughter of my father's only son." How is the girl related to Ravi?
     A) Sister
     B) Daughter
     C) Niece
     D) Cousin
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) Daughter
     Explanation: Ravi's father's only son is Ravi himself. Therefore, the girl is Ravi's daughter.

Q03. Choose the word closest in meaning to ANCIENT:
     A) Modern
     B) Recent
     C) Fresh
     D) Old
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) Old
     Explanation: ANCIENT means belonging to times long past; old.

Q04. Choose the word opposite in meaning to VICTORY:
     A) Win
     B) Success
     C) Defeat
     D) Triumph
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) Defeat
     Explanation: The antonym of victory is defeat.

Q05. Find the next letter: B, E, H, K, ?
     A) J
     B) L
     C) M
     D) N
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) N
     Explanation: Pattern: Each letter advances by 3 positions in the alphabet: B(+3) → E(+3) → H(+3) → K(+3) → N.

Q06. ₹900 is divided between A and B in the ratio 2:1. How much does A get?
     A) ₹300
     B) ₹450
     C) ₹600
     D) ₹700
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) ₹600
     Explanation: Total parts = 2 + 1 = 3. A's share = 900 × (2/3) = ₹600.

Q07. A rectangular garden is 12 m long and 5 m wide. What is its perimeter?
     A) 17 m
     B) 34 m
     C) 60 m
     D) 120 m
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 34 m
     Explanation: Formula: Perimeter = 2 × (length + width) = 2 × (12 + 5) = 2 × 17 = 34 m.

Q08. A student's marks: Maths 70, Science 80, English 60, Social 90. What is the average mark?
     A) 75
     B) 80
     C) 85
     D) 90
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) 75
     Explanation: Total marks = 70 + 80 + 60 + 90 = 300. Average = 300 / 4 = 75.

Q09. In a class of 50 students, Priya's rank from the top is 18. What is her rank from the bottom?
     A) 31
     B) 32
     C) 33
     D) 34
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 33
     Explanation: Formula: Rank from bottom = Total students − Rank from top + 1 = 50 − 18 + 1 = 33.

Q10. Find the odd one out:
     A) Dog
     B) Eagle
     C) Cat
     D) Cow
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) Eagle
     Explanation: Dog, cat, and cow are mammals, whereas eagle is a bird.


SECTION 2: LEVEL 2 – INTERMEDIATE APTITUDE (QUESTIONS 11 - 20)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q11. 6 men can complete a work in 10 days. How many days will 12 men take to complete the same work?
     A) 4 days
     B) 5 days
     C) 6 days
     D) 8 days
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 5 days
     Explanation: Total work = 6 × 10 = 60 man-days. Days required by 12 men = 60 / 12 = 5 days.

Q12. In an exam, 60% of the 250 students passed. How many students failed?
     A) 80
     B) 90
     C) 100
     D) 120
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 100
     Explanation: Failed percentage = 100% − 60% = 40%. Failed students = 40% of 250 = (40 / 100) × 250 = 100.

Q13. A car goes from A to B at 40 km/h and returns at 60 km/h along the same route. What is its average speed for the whole trip?
     A) 48 km/h
     B) 50 km/h
     C) 52 km/h
     D) 54 km/h
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) 48 km/h
     Explanation: Harmonic mean formula: Average speed = 2xy / (x + y) = (2 × 40 × 60) / (40 + 60) = 4800 / 100 = 48 km/h.

Q14. Find the next number: 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ?
     A) 33
     B) 35
     C) 36
     D) 37
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) 37
     Explanation: Differences between successive terms: +3, +5, +7, +9, +11. Thus, 26 + 11 = 37 (or n² + 1 pattern for n = 1 to 6).

Q15. Statements: All cats are animals. All animals are living beings.
     Conclusions: I. All cats are living beings. II. All living beings are cats.
     A) Only I follows
     B) Only II follows
     C) Both I and II follow
     D) Neither follows
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Only I follows
     Explanation: All cats are animals and all animals are living beings implies all cats are living beings (Conclusion I follows). Conclusion II is the converse and does not follow.

Q16. A sum of ₹5,000 earns ₹600 as simple interest in 2 years. What is the rate of interest per annum?
     A) 5%
     B) 6%
     C) 8%
     D) 10%
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 6%
     Explanation: Formula: R = (SI × 100) / (P × T) = (600 × 100) / (5000 × 2) = 60,000 / 10,000 = 6%.

Q17. Meena walks 10 m east, turns left and walks 5 m, then turns left again and walks 10 m. How far is she from her starting point?
     A) 0 m
     B) 5 m
     C) 10 m
     D) 15 m
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 5 m
     Explanation: Moving 10 m East, 5 m North, and then 10 m West cancels the horizontal movement, leaving her 5 m North of her starting point.

Q18. A and B invest ₹6,000 and ₹4,000 in a business. If the profit is ₹5,000, what is A's share?
     A) ₹2,000
     B) ₹2,500
     C) ₹3,000
     D) ₹3,500
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) ₹3,000
     Explanation: Profit ratio = 6000 : 4000 = 3 : 2. A's share = 5000 × (3 / 5) = ₹3,000.

Q19. One card is drawn at random from a standard pack of 52 cards. What is the probability that it is a king?
     A) 1/4
     B) 1/26
     C) 1/52
     D) 1/13
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) 1/13
     Explanation: There are 4 kings in a deck of 52 cards. Probability = 4 / 52 = 1/13.

Q20. Pen : Write :: Knife : ?
     A) Sharp
     B) Kitchen
     C) Cut
     D) Steel
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) Cut
     Explanation: A pen is used to write; analogously, a knife is used to cut.


SECTION 3: LEVEL 3 – ADVANCED APTITUDE (QUESTIONS 21 - 30)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q21. Two successive discounts of 20% and 10% are equal to a single discount of:
     A) 25%
     B) 28%
     C) 30%
     D) 32%
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 28%
     Explanation: Equivalent discount = d1 + d2 − (d1 × d2)/100 = 20 + 10 − (20 × 10)/100 = 30 − 2 = 28%.

Q22. A can complete a work in 12 days and B in 24 days. They work together for 4 days and then A leaves. How many more days will B take to finish the remaining work?
     A) 8 days
     B) 10 days
     C) 12 days
     D) 16 days
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 12 days
     Explanation: (A + B)'s 1-day work = 1/12 + 1/24 = 3/24 = 1/8. In 4 days, work completed = 4 × (1/8) = 1/2. Remaining work = 1/2. Days for B = (1/2) / (1/24) = 12 days.

Q23. Four friends A, B, C and D sit around a square table facing the centre. A sits opposite C, and B sits to the right of A. Who sits to the left of A?
     A) Person D
     B) Person B
     C) Person C
     D) Cannot be determined
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Person D
     Explanation: Facing centre, A is opposite C. Since B is to A's right, the seat to A's left must be occupied by Person D.

Q24. A pipe can fill a tank in 6 hours. Because of a leak, it takes 8 hours. How long will the leak alone take to empty the full tank?
     A) 12 hours
     B) 24 hours
     C) 36 hours
     D) 48 hours
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 24 hours
     Explanation: Leak rate = 1/6 − 1/8 = (4 − 3)/24 = 1/24 per hour. Hence, the leak alone will take 24 hours to empty the tank.

Q25. In what ratio must rice at ₹30/kg be mixed with rice at ₹45/kg to get a mixture worth ₹35/kg?
     A) 1:2
     B) 1:1
     C) 3:2
     D) 2:1
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) 2:1
     Explanation: By rule of alligation: (Cost of dearer − Mean price) : (Mean price − Cost of cheaper) = (45 − 35) : (35 − 30) = 10 : 5 = 2 : 1.

Q26. In how many different ways can the letters of the word LEVEL be arranged?
     A) 20
     B) 25
     C) 30
     D) 60
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 30
     Explanation: The word LEVEL has 5 letters with L appearing 2 times and E appearing 2 times: 5! / (2! × 2!) = 120 / (2 × 2) = 30 ways.

Q27. In a pie chart of a family's budget, Salary is shown by 120°, Rent by 90° and Others by 150°. If the total expense is ₹36,000, how much is spent on Rent?
     A) ₹9,000
     B) ₹10,000
     C) ₹12,000
     D) ₹15,000
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) ₹9,000
     Explanation: Rent angle = 90°. Total angle = 360°. Rent expenditure = (90 / 360) × 36,000 = (1/4) × 36,000 = ₹9,000.

Q28. If 5 + 3 = 28, 9 + 1 = 810 and 8 + 6 = 214, then 7 + 4 = ?
     A) 113
     B) 411
     C) 314
     D) 311
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) 311
     Explanation: Pattern: (first − second) concatenated with (first + second). 5 − 3 = 2, 5 + 3 = 8 → 28. 9 − 1 = 8, 9 + 1 = 10 → 810. For 7 + 4: 7 − 4 = 3, 7 + 4 = 11 → 311.

Q29. If 5th March 2024 was a Tuesday, what day of the week was 5th March 2025?
     A) Tuesday
     B) Wednesday
     C) Thursday
     D) Friday
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) Wednesday
     Explanation: Total days from 5 March 2024 to 5 March 2025 is 365 days (February 2025 has 28 days). 365 mod 7 = 1 odd day. Tuesday + 1 = Wednesday.

Q30. Choose the correct word to fill the blank: She has been working in this company ______ 2019.
     A) for
     B) from
     C) by
     D) since
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) since
     Explanation: "Since" is used to refer to a fixed starting point in time (2019), whereas "for" is used with a duration.
