SECTION 1: LEVEL 1 – BASIC APTITUDE (QUESTIONS 01 - 10)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q01. What is 25% of 200?
     A) 25
     B) 40
     C) 50
     D) 75
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 50
     Explanation: 25% of 200 = (25 / 100) * 200 = 50

Q02. A person buys a pen for ₹20 and sells it for ₹25. What is the profit?
     A) ₹3
     B) ₹5
     C) ₹7
     D) ₹10
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) ₹5
     Explanation: Profit = Selling Price - Cost Price = 25 - 20 = ₹5

Q03. Find the next number: 2, 4, 6, 8, ?
     A) 9
     B) 10
     C) 11
     D) 12
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 10
     Explanation: Even numbers sequence (+2 increment each step)

Q04. If 5 books cost ₹250, what is the cost of 1 book?
     A) ₹40
     B) ₹45
     C) ₹50
     D) ₹55
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) ₹50
     Explanation: Cost of 1 book = ₹250 / 5 = ₹50

Q05. A train travels 60 km in 1 hour. How far will it travel in 3 hours?
     A) 120 km
     B) 150 km
     C) 180 km
     D) 200 km
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 180 km
     Explanation: Distance = Speed * Time = 60 km/h * 3 h = 180 km

Q06. Find the average of 10, 20 and 30.
     A) 15
     B) 20
     C) 25
     D) 30
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 20
     Explanation: Average = (10 + 20 + 30) / 3 = 60 / 3 = 20

Q07. If today is Monday, what day will it be after 3 days?
     A) Tuesday
     B) Wednesday
     C) Thursday
     D) Friday
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) Thursday
     Explanation: Monday + 3 days = Thursday

Q08. A number is increased from 100 to 120. What is the percentage increase?
     A) 10%
     B) 15%
     C) 20%
     D) 25%
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 20%
     Explanation: Percentage increase = ((120 - 100) / 100) * 100 = 20%

Q09. Find the odd one out:
     A) Apple
     B) Mango
     C) Banana
     D) Carrot
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) Carrot
     Explanation: Carrot is a root vegetable; Apple, Mango, and Banana are fruits

Q10. If 10 workers complete a work in 6 days, which of the following is a reasonable conclusion?
     A) More workers may complete it faster
     B) More workers always take more time
     C) Workers cannot work together
     D) Number of workers does not matter
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) More workers may complete it faster
     Explanation: Total work is constant; increasing workforce increases rate of work

SECTION 2: LEVEL 2 – INTERMEDIATE APTITUDE (QUESTIONS 11 - 20)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q11. A product costs ₹800. It is sold at a 15% profit. What is the selling price?
     A) ₹880
     B) ₹900
     C) ₹920
     D) ₹950
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) ₹920
     Explanation: Profit = 15% of 800 = 120. SP = 800 + 120 = ₹920

Q12. The average of 5 numbers is 24. What is their total?
     A) 100
     B) 110
     C) 120
     D) 125
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 120
     Explanation: Total = Average * Count = 24 * 5 = 120

Q13. A car travels at 60 km/h. How much time will it take to travel 150 km?
     A) 2 hours
     B) 2.5 hours
     C) 3 hours
     D) 3.5 hours
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 2.5 hours
     Explanation: Time = Distance / Speed = 150 / 60 = 2.5 hours

Q14. Find the next number: 3, 6, 12, 24, ?
     A) 36
     B) 42
     C) 48
     D) 54
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 48
     Explanation: Each term is multiplied by 2 (Geometric progression: 24 * 2 = 48)

Q15. A shop gives a 20% discount on an item marked ₹1,500. What is the selling price?
     A) ₹1,100
     B) ₹1,200
     C) ₹1,250
     D) ₹1,300
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) ₹1,200
     Explanation: Discount = 20% of 1,500 = 300. SP = 1,500 - 300 = ₹1,200

Q16. The ratio of boys to girls in a class is 3:2. If there are 30 boys, how many girls are there?
     A) 15
     B) 20
     C) 25
     D) 30
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 20
     Explanation: 1 ratio unit = 30 / 3 = 10. Girls = 2 * 10 = 20

Q17. A can complete a work in 10 days and B can complete it in 15 days. Working together, approximately how many days will they take?
     A) 5 days
     B) 6 days
     C) 7 days
     D) 8 days
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 6 days
     Explanation: (A * B) / (A + B) = (10 * 15) / (10 + 15) = 150 / 25 = 6 days

Q18. If 40% of a number is 80, what is the number?
     A) 160
     B) 180
     C) 200
     D) 240
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 200
     Explanation: Number = 80 / 0.40 = 200

Q19. Find the missing number: 5, 10, 20, 40, ?
     A) 60
     B) 70
     C) 80
     D) 100
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 80
     Explanation: Each term is multiplied by 2 (40 * 2 = 80)

Q20. A person walks 5 km north and then 3 km east. In which direction is he from his starting point?
     A) North-West
     B) South-East
     C) North-East
     D) South-West
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) North-East
     Explanation: From origin, moving North (+y) then East (+x) places the person in North-East

SECTION 3: LEVEL 3 – ADVANCED APTITUDE (QUESTIONS 21 - 30)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q21. A number is increased by 20% and then decreased by 20%. What is the overall percentage change?
     A) No change
     B) 2% decrease
     C) 4% decrease
     D) 4% increase
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 4% decrease
     Explanation: Net change = +20 - 20 - (20 * 20 / 100) = -4% (4% decrease)

Q22. A and B can complete a work in 12 and 18 days respectively. If they work together, how many days will they approximately take?
     A) 6.2 days
     B) 7.2 days
     C) 8.2 days
     D) 9.2 days
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 7.2 days
     Explanation: (12 * 18) / (12 + 18) = 216 / 30 = 7.2 days

Q23. A train 120 metres long crosses a pole in 6 seconds. What is its speed?
     A) 15 m/s
     B) 20 m/s
     C) 25 m/s
     D) 30 m/s
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 20 m/s
     Explanation: Speed = Distance / Time = 120 m / 6 s = 20 m/s

Q24. The ratio of A:B is 4:5 and B:C is 10:7. What is A:C?
     A) 4:7
     B) 8:7
     C) 5:7
     D) 8:9
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 8:7
     Explanation: A/C = (A/B) * (B/C) = (4/5) * (10/7) = 40/35 = 8/7

Q25. A man spends 70% of his salary. If he saves ₹9,000, what is his salary?
     A) ₹27,000
     B) ₹30,000
     C) ₹32,000
     D) ₹35,000
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) ₹30,000
     Explanation: Savings = 100% - 70% = 30%. Salary = 9,000 / 0.30 = ₹30,000

Q26. A box contains 5 red, 3 blue and 2 green balls. What is the probability of picking a blue ball?
     A) 1/5
     B) 3/10
     C) 1/3
     D) 2/5
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 3/10
     Explanation: Total balls = 5 + 3 + 2 = 10. Blue balls = 3. P(Blue) = 3/10

Q27. Find the next number: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
     A) 36
     B) 40
     C) 42
     D) 44
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 42
     Explanation: Pattern: 1*2 = 2, 2*3 = 6, 3*4 = 12, 4*5 = 20, 5*6 = 30, 6*7 = 42

Q28. If the selling price of an item is ₹1,080 after a 10% discount, what was the marked price?
     A) ₹1,150
     B) ₹1,180
     C) ₹1,200
     D) ₹1,250
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) ₹1,200
     Explanation: SP = 90% of MP => 1,080 = 0.90 * MP => MP = 1,080 / 0.90 = ₹1,200

Q29. A boat travels 30 km downstream in 2 hours and the same distance upstream in 3 hours. What is the speed of the boat in still water?
     A) 10 km/h
     B) 12.5 km/h
     C) 15 km/h
     D) 20 km/h
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 12.5 km/h
     Explanation: Downstream speed = 30/2 = 15 km/h; Upstream speed = 30/3 = 10 km/h; Still-water speed = (15 + 10) / 2 = 12.5 km/h

Q30. A team has 6 members. If each member shakes hands with every other member exactly once, how many handshakes will occur?
     A) 12
     B) 15
     C) 18
     D) 20
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 15
     Explanation: Formula: n(n - 1) / 2 = 6 * 5 / 2 = 15
