SECTION 1: LEVEL 1 – BASIC APTITUDE (QUESTIONS 01 - 10)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q01. If CAT is coded as DBU (each letter moved one step forward), how is DOG coded?
     A) EOH
     B) DPH
     C) EPH
     D) EPG
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) EPH
     Explanation: D→E, O→P, G→H, so DOG = EPH

Q02. Pointing to a man, Meena says, "He is the son of my mother's only brother." How is the man related to Meena?
     A) Brother
     B) Cousin
     C) Uncle
     D) Nephew
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) Cousin
     Explanation: Mother's brother = maternal uncle; his son = cousin

Q03. Choose the word closest in meaning to RAPID:
     A) Slow
     B) Weak
     C) Late
     D) Fast
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) Fast
     Explanation: RAPID means very fast, swift, or quick.

Q04. Choose the word opposite in meaning to EXPAND:
     A) Contract
     B) Increase
     C) Grow
     D) Stretch
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Contract
     Explanation: Expand means to enlarge; its exact opposite is contract.

Q05. Find the next letter: A, C, E, G, ?
     A) H
     B) I
     C) J
     D) K
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) I
     Explanation: Pattern: Alternate letters with +2 step: A(+2)→C(+2)→E(+2)→G(+2)→I

Q06. What is the simple interest on ₹1,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years?
     A) ₹100
     B) ₹150
     C) ₹200
     D) ₹250
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) ₹200
     Explanation: Formula: SI = (P × R × T) / 100 = (1000 × 10 × 2) / 100 = ₹200

Q07. By how many degrees does the minute hand of a clock move in 10 minutes?
     A) 60°
     B) 30°
     C) 45°
     D) 90°
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) 60°
     Explanation: The minute hand covers 360° in 60 min (6°/min). For 10 min: 10 × 6° = 60°

Q08. In a non-leap year, if 1st January is a Monday, what day is 1st February?
     A) Thursday
     B) Tuesday
     C) Wednesday
     D) Friday
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Thursday
     Explanation: January has 31 days = 4 weeks + 3 odd days. Monday + 3 days = Thursday

Q09. Units sold in a week: Mon – 40, Tue – 55, Wed – 35, Thu – 60, Fri – 50. On which day were the most units sold?
     A) Monday
     B) Tuesday
     C) Wednesday
     D) Thursday
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) Thursday
     Explanation: Comparing sales figures: Mon(40), Tue(55), Wed(35), Thu(60), Fri(50). Thu is highest with 60.

Q10. Arun is taller than Bala. Bala is taller than Charan. Who is the shortest?
     A) Arun
     B) Charan
     C) Bala
     D) Cannot be determined
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) Charan
     Explanation: Height hierarchy: Arun > Bala > Charan. Therefore, Charan is the shortest.


SECTION 2: LEVEL 2 – INTERMEDIATE APTITUDE (QUESTIONS 11 - 20)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q11. A father is 3 times as old as his son. After 10 years, the father will be twice as old as the son. What is the son's present age?
     A) 8 years
     B) 10 years
     C) 12 years
     D) 15 years
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 10 years
     Explanation: Let son = S, father = 3S. 3S + 10 = 2(S + 10) => 3S + 10 = 2S + 20 => S = 10 years.

Q12. Statements: All pens are books. Some books are bags.
     Conclusions: I. Some pens are bags. II. Some bags are books.
     A) Only I follows
     B) Only II follows
     C) Both I and II follow
     D) Neither follows
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) Only II follows
     Explanation: Conclusion II is the direct converse of "Some books are bags", so it definitely follows. Conclusion I does not necessarily follow.

Q13. What is the compound interest on ₹10,000 at 10% per annum for 2 years?
     A) ₹2,000
     B) ₹2,200
     C) ₹2,100
     D) ₹2,400
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) ₹2,100
     Explanation: Amount = 10,000 × (1 + 10/100)² = 10,000 × 1.21 = ₹12,100. CI = ₹12,100 - ₹10,000 = ₹2,100.

Q14. A 20-litre mixture contains milk and water in the ratio 3:1. How much water must be added to make the ratio 1:1?
     A) 10 litres
     B) 5 litres
     C) 8 litres
     D) 15 litres
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) 10 litres
     Explanation: Milk = 20 × (3/4) = 15 L, Water = 5 L. For ratio 1:1, water must equal 15 L. Added water = 15 - 5 = 10 L.

Q15. Ravi is facing North. He turns right and then turns right again. Which direction is he facing now?
     A) East
     B) West
     C) North
     D) South
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) South
     Explanation: Facing North → 1st right turn: East → 2nd right turn: South.

Q16. Find the next number: 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, ?
     A) 18
     B) 20
     C) 21
     D) 24
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 21
     Explanation: Fibonacci series (each number is sum of preceding two): 2+3=5, 3+5=8, 5+8=13, 8+13=21.

Q17. Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : ?
     A) School
     B) Student
     C) Book
     D) Class
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) School
     Explanation: A doctor works in a hospital; similarly, a teacher works in a school.

Q18. A family's monthly expenses: Food 40%, Rent 25%, Education 15%, Others 20%. If the monthly income is ₹40,000, how much is spent on rent?
     A) ₹8,000
     B) ₹10,000
     C) ₹12,000
     D) ₹15,000
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) ₹10,000
     Explanation: Rent = 25% of ₹40,000 = (25 / 100) × 40,000 = ₹10,000.

Q19. In how many different ways can 4 different books be arranged on a shelf?
     A) 12
     B) 16
     C) 48
     D) 24
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) 24
     Explanation: Permutation of 4 distinct items = 4! = 4 × 3 × 2 × 1 = 24 ways.

Q20. Find the odd one out: 3, 5, 9, 11
     A) 9
     B) 3
     C) 5
     D) 11
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) 9
     Explanation: 3, 5, and 11 are prime numbers; 9 is a composite number (divisible by 3).


SECTION 3: LEVEL 3 – ADVANCED APTITUDE (QUESTIONS 21 - 30)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q21. What is the angle between the hour hand and the minute hand at 4:30?
     A) 30°
     B) 45°
     C) 60°
     D) 75°
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 45°
     Explanation: Hour hand position at 4:30 = 4 × 30° + 30 × 0.5° = 135°. Minute hand = 30 × 6° = 180°. Angle = |180° - 135°| = 45°.

Q22. Five friends P, Q, R, S and T sit in a row. P is at the left end, Q sits next to P, T is at the right end, and R sits between Q and S. Who sits in the middle?
     A) Q
     B) S
     C) R
     D) T
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) R
     Explanation: Order from left to right: P, Q, R, S, T. The 3rd (middle) person is R.

Q23. A sells an article to B at a 20% profit. B sells it to C at a 10% loss. If C pays ₹1,080, what did A pay for it?
     A) ₹1,100
     B) ₹900
     C) ₹1,200
     D) ₹1,000
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) ₹1,000
     Explanation: B's CP = 1,080 / 0.9 = ₹1,200. A's CP = 1,200 / 1.2 = ₹1,000.

Q24. Two trains of lengths 100 m and 150 m run on parallel tracks in opposite directions at 30 m/s and 20 m/s. How long will they take to cross each other?
     A) 5 seconds
     B) 4 seconds
     C) 6 seconds
     D) 10 seconds
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) 5 seconds
     Explanation: Relative speed = 30 + 20 = 50 m/s. Total distance = 100 + 150 = 250 m. Time = 250 / 50 = 5 seconds.

Q25. Statements: No engineer is lazy. Some students are engineers. Which conclusion definitely follows?
     A) No student is an engineer
     B) All engineers are students
     C) All students are lazy
     D) Some students are not lazy
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) Some students are not lazy
     Explanation: The students who are engineers cannot be lazy (as no engineer is lazy). Hence, some students are not lazy.

Q26. Two dice are rolled together. What is the probability that the sum of the numbers is 7?
     A) 1/12
     B) 1/9
     C) 1/6
     D) 5/36
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 1/6
     Explanation: Favourable outcomes = (1,6), (2,5), (3,4), (4,3), (5,2), (6,1) = 6 pairs out of 36. Probability = 6/36 = 1/6.

Q27. Two bells ring at intervals of 12 and 18 minutes. If they ring together at 9:00 AM, when will they next ring together?
     A) 9:24 AM
     B) 9:36 AM
     C) 9:30 AM
     D) 9:48 AM
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 9:36 AM
     Explanation: LCM(12, 18) = 36 minutes. Next simultaneous ring = 9:00 AM + 36 min = 9:36 AM.

Q28. A company's yearly profit: 2021 – ₹50 lakh, 2022 – ₹60 lakh, 2023 – ₹72 lakh. What is the percentage growth in profit from 2022 to 2023?
     A) 20%
     B) 12%
     C) 15%
     D) 24%
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) 20%
     Explanation: Growth % = ((72 - 60) / 60) × 100 = (12 / 60) × 100 = 20%.

Q29. A is B's sister. C is B's mother. D is C's father. E is D's mother. How is A related to D?
     A) Grandmother
     B) Daughter
     C) Sister
     D) Granddaughter
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: D) Granddaughter
     Explanation: C is mother of A and B. D is father of C. Therefore, D is grandfather to A, and A is D's granddaughter.

Q30. Choose the grammatically correct sentence:
     A) Each of the students have submitted the form.
     B) Each of the students has submitted the form.
     C) Each of the student have submitted the form.
     D) Each of the student has submitted the form.
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) Each of the students has submitted the form.
     Explanation: "Each" takes a singular verb ("has"), while the prepositional phrase "of the" requires the plural noun "students".
