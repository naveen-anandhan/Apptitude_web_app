Q01. What is 15% of 200?
     A) 20
     B) 25
     C) 30

     A) 120 km
     B) 140 km
     C) 150 km
     D) 160 km
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: C) 150 km

Q03. If A can complete a work in 10 days and B in 15 days, how many days will they take working together?
     A) 5 days
     B) 6 days
     C) 7.5 days
     D) 8 days
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: B) 6 days

     A) 20%
     B) 25%
     C) 30%

SECTION 2: MECHANICAL ENGINEERING - CATIA (V5) SPECIALIZATION (QUESTIONS 21 - 30)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q21. Which company is the original developer and publisher of the CATIA software suite?
     A) Dassault Systèmes
     B) Autodesk
     C) Siemens Digital Industries
     D) PTC
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Dassault Systèmes

Q22. Which workbench in CATIA V5 is primarily used for creating 3D parametric solid features?
     A) Part Design
     B) Generative Shape Design
     C) Drafting
     D) DMU Kinematics
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Part Design

Q23. In CATIA Part Design, which command is used to extrude a 2D closed profile linearly into a 3D solid?
     A) Pad
     B) Shaft
     C) Pocket
     D) Rib
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Pad

Q24. In CATIA Part Design, which command creates a solid of revolution by revolving a profile around a center axis?
     A) Shaft
     B) Pad
     C) Groove
     D) Slot
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Shaft

Q25. Which command in CATIA Part Design removes material by cutting into an existing solid with a 2D sketch profile?
     A) Pocket
     B) Pad
     C) Hole
     D) Multi-sections Solid
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Pocket

Q26. Which CATIA workbench is widely utilized for complex automotive and aerospace class-A surface modeling?
     A) Generative Shape Design (GSD)
     B) Assembly Design
     C) Structure Design
     D) Weld Design
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Generative Shape Design (GSD)

Q27. What is the 3D on-screen manipulation instrument in CATIA used for dragging, orienting, and positioning parts called?
     A) Compass
     B) Protractor
     C) Cursor Axis
     D) Orbit Wheel
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Compass

Q28. What is the standard native file extension for a single 3D solid part model in CATIA V5?
     A) .CATPart
     B) .CATProduct
     C) .CATDrawing
     D) .prt
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) .CATPart

Q29. What is the standard native file extension for an assembly of multiple components in CATIA V5?
     A) .CATProduct
     B) .CATPart
     C) .CATProcess
     D) .asm
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) .CATProduct

Q30. In CATIA Part Design, which dress-up feature is used to slant faces at a specified angle for mold release?
     A) Draft Angle
     B) Chamfer
     C) Edge Fillet
     D) Shell
     >> CORRECT ANSWER: A) Draft Angle
